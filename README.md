Roguelite Adventure

🎮 Overview

Roguelite Adventure is an action-packed Unity game where players embark on a thrilling journey, battling waves of monsters to gain experience. As players level up, they can choose new weapons or passive skills to enhance their abilities. The game ends when the player's health is fully depleted.

🚀 Features

Dynamic Combat System: Engage in fast-paced battles against various monsters.

Leveling Mechanic: Gain experience from defeating enemies and level up to unlock new weapons and passive skills.

Weapon and Skill Selection: Choose from a variety of weapons and abilities to customize your playstyle.

Permadeath Mechanic: The game concludes when the player's health reaches zero.

🛠️ How to Play

Start the Game: Launch the game from the Unity build.

Battle Monsters: Defeat enemies to gain experience.

Level Up: Choose from a selection of weapons or passive skills to enhance your character.

Survive as Long as Possible: Your goal is to stay alive and become more powerful with each level-up.

Game Over: The game ends when your health reaches zero.

🎯 Controls

WASD / Arrow Keys: Move the character.

Mouse pointer: Auto attack.

ESC: Pause menu.
